How we work

A Florida staffing company that charges workers nothing

Abdalla Staffing LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L24000483780, filed in 2024, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

The commitments that matter. We charge workers no fee for anything — placement, registration, training, transport, equipment or screening — and any agency that does is one to report. Workers are our employees on our payroll with taxes withheld, not contractors, and they are covered by our workers compensation policy. Eligibility is verified through I-9 and E-Verify. Overtime is paid under the FLSA on all hours worked. Site safety, supervision and equipment remain the client's responsibility at its premises.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do I have to pay to register for work?

No. Not for registration, placement, training, equipment, transport or background screening — not for anything, ever. An agency asking a worker for money is doing something you should report.

Am I an employee or a contractor?

Our employee. You are on our payroll with taxes withheld and covered by our workers compensation policy. We do not classify warehouse workers as independent contractors.

Who is responsible if someone is injured on our site?

Workers are on our comp policy and we provide the certificate. Site safety, supervision and equipment remain yours as the site operator, and for some purposes we may both be treated as joint employers.

Will overtime be billed correctly?

Yes, calculated under the FLSA on all hours worked. Agencies that quietly reset hours across assignments to avoid overtime are creating a liability for their clients as well as cheating workers.

How much notice do you need for seasonal staff?

As much as you can give — six weeks lets us recruit and induct properly. Calling during peak week gets you whoever is available, which rarely ends well.

Do you background check everyone?

Only where the role requires it, with written consent as the FCRA requires and with the proper adverse action process. We do not screen indiscriminately.

Need people, or need work?

Clients get a reply within four hours. Workers register free, always.
